Apprenticeship Program

Our One-Year Maritime Apprenticeship Program is a 12-month structured shipboard training program for qualified students or graduates of BS Marine Transportation (BSMT) and BS Marine Engineering (BSMarE), run in line with MARINA, CHED, and STCW requirements.

Duration12 months of supervised shipboard training and sea service, documented through the apprentice's Training Record Book (TRB)
Deck Apprentices (BSMT)Trained in the Deck Department — bridge watchkeeping, navigation, cargo operations, mooring, safety and emergency procedures, and related competencies
Engine Apprentices (BSMarE)Trained in the Engine Department — main/auxiliary engine operations, machinery maintenance, engine-room watchkeeping, and related competencies
SupervisionMaster, Chief Engineer, and other qualified officers, depending on department
Upon completionEligible apprentices may receive a Certificate of Sea Service / Training, subject to school and MARINA/STCW requirements. Completion does not by itself guarantee employment or licensure.

Application Requirements & How to Apply

Applicants must submit a complete set of documentary requirements, including: endorsement letter from school, updated resume, Seafarer's Identification and Record Book (SIRB) with 2 certified true copies, Seaman's Log Book, Basic Training (BT) and SDSD certificates, Crowd and Crisis Control Management certificate, Watchkeeping Training certificate, Transcript of Records, drug test (valid 6 months), medical certificate from a DOH-accredited clinic, NBI Clearance, and 3 2x2 photos in marine merchant uniform.

How to apply: Scan all requirements into one PDF named SURNAME, GIVEN NAME, MIDDLE INITIAL, then email to pscsc.recruitment@yahoo.com. You may also apply in person at the Head Office (Maharlika Highway, New San Roque, Pili, Camarines Sur, inside Partido Rice Mill). An apprenticeship fund of PHP 10,000 applies, covering PPE, dental, and insurance. For inquiries, call (+63)997-387-0769 or message "HR Gellie" on Facebook Messenger.
