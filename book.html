Book Your Trip

How to book passage or cargo space with Santa Clara & Penafrancia Shipping

Passenger Booking

Most passengers travel on a walk-in basis, though advance booking is available directly at our terminals.

  1. Head to the terminalArrive at the port for your route (see our list of ports). Walk-in booking is available and is how most passengers travel with us.
  2. Prefer to book ahead?Currently, advanced passenger bookings can only be done through 12GO (extra fees may apply). Coming soon: We're partnering with Mercury Transport so you can book and pay online!

Cargo Booking

Trucks and cargo shipments follow a separate, advance-payment process.

  1. Message our Cargo Viber GroupSend a message to our official cargo booking Viber group to arrange your shipment.
  2. Send advance paymentPay via InstaPay or QR PH to secure your booking.
  3. Book your ferry slotOnce payment is confirmed, coordinate with our staff in the Viber group and we'll reserve your slot on the next available cargo sailing, or on your preferred date.
  4. Drop off & timingCoordinate drop-off at the shipyard/terminal with our staff in the Viber group ahead of the scheduled departure.
