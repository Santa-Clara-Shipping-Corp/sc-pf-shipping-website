About Us

Our story, our sister companies, and what drives us

Two Companies, One Fleet

Santa Clara Shipping Corporation and Penafrancia Shipping Corporation are separately incorporated sister companies under common ownership and management, based in Bicol. In practice they operate as a single fleet — sharing crews, schedules, ports, and terminals — a common structure among Philippine domestic shipping operators that keeps operations resilient and consistent for passengers and shippers alike.

Santa Clara Shipping Corporation

A domestic shipping corporation duly organized under the laws of the Philippines, established in 1996.

Penafrancia Shipping Corporation

Established 2006, Penafrancia Shipping Corporation shares Santa Clara's routes, ports, and management team, and operates several vessels that trace their roots to an earlier fleet acquisition in the Bicol region.

Our History

Compiled from company records and a 2019 feature by the Philippine Ship Spotters Society. Dates should be verified with company records before publishing.

1996

Santa Clara Shipping Corporation is founded

Established in Pili, Camarines Sur, amid demand from travelers and shippers across the San Bernardino Strait for better, more reliable ferry service after several incumbent operators declined or exited the route.

1999

First two vessels take to sea

M/V King Frederick and M/V Nelvin Jules begin sailing the Matnog–Allen crossing, offering newer, faster, better-appointed service than the aging competition.

2004

Fleet grows with M/V Hansel Jobett

A third vessel joins the Matnog–Allen route as demand continues to grow.

2006

Penafrancia Shipping's fleet lineage begins

Following an amicable settlement with a competing operator, a fleet of vessels (later renamed M/V Eugene Elson, M/V Don Benito Ambrosio II, and M/V Don Herculano) is transferred into what becomes Penafrancia Shipping Corporation, expanding the group's presence on the Tabaco–Virac route.

2007–2017

Fleet modernization and expansion

Additional vessels join the combined fleet — including M/V Mac Bryan, M/V Anthon Raphael, M/V Nathan Matthew, M/V Jack Daniel, M/V Adrian Jude, and M/V Almirante Federico — alongside the construction of Jubasan Port in Allen, Northern Samar, the group's first privately-owned port.

2017–2018

Entry into cargo RORO service

LCT Aldain Dowey and LCT ST 888 join the fleet, giving trucks and cargo their own dedicated vessels separate from passenger sailings.

2022–2023

Continued growth

The Hilongos, Leyte route opens in 2022, and the newly-built M/V General Santos joins the fleet in 2023, reflecting continued investment in the Visayas and Mindanao network.
