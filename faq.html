Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about routes, booking, and policies

What are your routes and schedules?
See our full Routes & Schedule on the homepage.
How can I book a trip?
Visit our Book Your Trip page for step-by-step instructions for both passengers and cargo.
What is your refund policy?
If you booked in advance online, your refund amount depends on how close to departure you cancel: 90% of the base fare if cancelled more than 48 hours before departure, 75% if cancelled within 48–24 hours, and 50% if cancelled within 24 hours of departure (a portion covers shipping-line and processing fees). If we need to cancel or reschedule your trip, you'll receive a full refund with no surcharge. If you booked directly at the port, this policy is still pending confirmation from staff — please contact our office directly (see Contact Us) to discuss a refund.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations follow the same tiers as our refund policy above, based on how close to departure you cancel. Please contact our office directly (see Contact Us) to process a cancellation.
Can I change the date of my booked trip?
If you booked in advance online, you can rebook to a new date within 3 months of your original booking, as long as you notify us at least 48 hours before your intended new departure. A 10% surcharge applies if you rebook more than 24 hours before your original scheduled trip, or 20% if within 24 hours. If we need to reschedule your trip, there's no surcharge. If you booked directly at the port, date changes currently aren't available — please contact our office directly to discuss options.
How can I suggest new routes?
We welcome route suggestions from travelers and shippers. Use the form on our homepage under "Suggest a New Route."
I have a complaint. How do I reach out?
Please contact our office directly (see Contact Us) by phone or email, and select "Complaint" as the subject if using our contact form.
Are you hiring at the moment?
See our Careers page for open roles, or reach out via email or through the contact form to inquire.
